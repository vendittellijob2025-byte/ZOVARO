# ZOVARO WORLDWIDE — Brand identity and rollout

## Approved brand architecture

- Full brand name: **ZOVARO WORLDWIDE SHOP & JOB**
- Short public-facing name: **ZOVARO WORLDWIDE**
- Descriptor: **SHOP & JOB**
- Brand promise: **Discover Better. Shop Smarter.**
- Primary website while the owned domain is pending: https://zovaro.vercel.app

Use “ZOVARO WORLDWIDE” as the dominant name in headers, social profiles and promotional material. Use “SHOP & JOB” as a supporting descriptor, not as a second competing logo or an unexplained string of keywords.

## Brand meaning and accurate positioning

ZOVARO is a global discovery and referral platform connecting people with third-party products, offers, services, jobs and commercial opportunities. For affiliate commerce, visitors are referred to the independent merchant or provider. ZOVARO does not own or stock products and does not process buyer payments, shipping, fulfilment, customer orders, refunds or returns.

Do not imply that every listed merchant, job or opportunity is verified, available worldwide, or endorsed by ZOVARO. Availability, eligibility, prices, commissions and terms depend on the relevant third party and must be checked before publication.

## Visual identity rules

- Preserve the exact original ZOVARO symbol and wordmark supplied by the owner. Do not redraw, replace, distort, recolour, crop or regenerate the symbol.
- Extend the lockup by adding **WORLDWIDE** as a supporting word element; keep **SHOP & JOB** as a smaller descriptor where space allows.
- Palette: deep navy, electric blue, white and restrained neutral greys.
- Style: clean, premium, modern, international, readable and responsive.
- Avoid visual clutter, fake trust seals, fabricated partner logos, unsupported “#1” claims, and stock imagery that could imply a real partner relationship.
- Use the existing approved logo asset as the source of truth: `assets/zovaro-logo.png`.

## Email identity

Use the role-based addresses and verification gates in [EMAIL-IDENTITY.md](./EMAIL-IDENTITY.md). Until a ZOVARO-owned domain and authenticated mailbox are available, do not send new cold outreach from a personal-looking sender or spoof a ZOVARO address. Drafts may be prepared but must remain unsent.

## Domain and cost gate

- Do not buy a domain, mailbox, subscription, paid social promotion or other service before the owner confirms that ZOVARO has received its first commissions and explicitly authorizes the expense.
- Do not use `zovaro.com`; it is not controlled by ZOVARO.
- Do not substitute `vendittelligames.com`; it belongs to a different brand.
- Keep `zovaro.vercel.app` live until an owned domain is actually acquired, configured and tested.
- Domain availability checks are not reservations and must be repeated at the time of any future purchase.

## Rollout sequence

1. Keep the current live site and original logo intact while preparing the brand kit.
2. Review header, footer, favicon, page metadata, social preview and mobile layouts against this identity.
3. Prepare platform-specific profile copy and visual sizes; verify actual account access and handle availability before claiming or changing anything.
4. Prepare the ZOVARO role-based email setup; activate addresses only after domain ownership, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and real delivery tests pass.
5. When the owner authorizes the domain purchase after commissions are received, configure it, verify HTTPS and email, then make the public identity changes together.
6. Test navigation, affiliate click tracking, contact links, metadata and mobile rendering after rollout.

## Approval boundaries

Routine, reversible preparation and technical checks may proceed. Do not accept contracts, exclusivity, legal or tax declarations, financial commitments, paid services, payout changes, or public statements about unverified partners without the owner's explicit approval.

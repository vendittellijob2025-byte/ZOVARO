# ZOVARO — Brand email identity and mailbox plan

## Mandatory sender policy

ZOVARO is the public-facing brand for all commercial, affiliate, partnership, support and media correspondence. Do not introduce ZOVARO as "vendittellijob" or use a personal-looking sender identity.

Until a ZOVARO-controlled domain and authenticated mailbox are verified, do not send new external outreach from the connected personal Gmail account. A Gmail display name such as "ZOVARO" does not hide the underlying sender address from recipients.

Do not invent addresses, spoof a From header, or use an unverified domain. Existing messages already sent from the connected Gmail account cannot be retroactively changed.

## Role addresses to provision on a verified ZOVARO-owned domain

Prefer one real mailbox plus aliases/forwarders (if the provider supports them at no additional cost), rather than paying for multiple inboxes.

- `partnerships@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — direct brand, retailer, restaurant, luxury, service and strategic partnership enquiries.
- `affiliates@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — affiliate networks, advertiser applications, tracking links, product feeds and programme support.
- `support@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — questions about the ZOVARO platform only. Product fulfilment, orders, refunds and returns remain the third-party merchant's responsibility.
- `info@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — general business correspondence and public contact.
- `media@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — press, creators, social-media collaborations and approved promotional assets.
- `finance@<verified-zovaro-domain>` — commission statements, reconciliation and partner payout administration; never use it to collect customer payments.

## Display names

Use role-specific display names, for example:
- ZOVARO Partnerships
- ZOVARO Affiliate Team
- ZOVARO Support
- ZOVARO Media
- ZOVARO Finance

Keep the legal/business identity accurate in signatures and agreements. Do not claim a legal entity, registered office, tax status, or contractual authority that has not been verified.

## Setup and verification gates

Before any address is used:
1. Confirm the domain is owned/controlled by ZOVARO and its renewal status is understood.
2. Confirm the email provider and plan are already included or explicitly approved; do not purchase upgrades or extra mailboxes without consent.
3. Create the primary mailbox and free aliases/forwarders where supported.
4. Configure and verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC; test inbound and outbound delivery.
5. Confirm the From name and actual From address shown to an external recipient.
6. Update website contact details, signatures, partner records and future outreach templates only after successful verification.

The current `zovaro.vercel.app` address is a website subdomain, not an email domain. The domain `vendittelligames.com` is not to be substituted for a ZOVARO-branded sender unless the user explicitly chooses that branding.

## Operating rule

Until all verification gates pass, prepare drafts and research contacts if useful, but do not send new cold outreach from the personal Gmail sender. Do not make commitments, accept contracts, exclusivity, spend, payout changes, or legal/tax representations without the user's explicit approval.

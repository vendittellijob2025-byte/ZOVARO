# ZOVARO Worldwide — Enquiry replies by customer language

## Mandatory language rule

Reply in the same language as the customer's latest enquiry. Detect the language from the message, not the sender's country or email domain. If the message is mixed-language, use the language of the main request. If genuinely unclear, ask briefly which language they prefer. Preserve the customer's currency and wording where practical, while always stating that prices are in US dollars.

Do not send an English-only response to a customer who wrote in Italian, Spanish, French, German or another language. For languages without a prepared template, translate the approved content accurately and keep all commercial terms unchanged.

Privacy Policy

ZOVARO helps visitors discover products, offers, job opportunities and community marketplace listings. This policy explains how information submitted through these features is handled.

Information users submit

When you submit a job opportunity or a product listing, ZOVARO processes the details you provide, which may include a name or company name, listing description, location, price or employment details, and a contact email address. Do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.

Moderation and publication

New job and product listings are stored in a private system and are not publicly visible while pending review. ZOVARO may approve or reject submissions to reduce spam, fraud, unsafe offers and misleading content. If you submit a listing, the contact email field is published only after review and only when you actively check the consent box confirming that it may be displayed publicly. Do not provide another person's contact details without their authorization.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to receive, review, display and administer listings, prevent abuse, and respond to service or safety issues. Technical information such as request metadata may be processed by the hosting and storage providers to operate and secure the service.

Storage and service providers

Marketplace submissions are stored in access-controlled private storage until approved. ZOVARO also uses hosting, storage, affiliate-network and social-media services. Those providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies. Approved listings and any contact email you expressly authorize for publication become visible to visitors.

Affiliate links and external websites

ZOVARO may display affiliate links to third-party merchants. When you follow a link, the relevant merchant or affiliate network may receive technical information needed to process the referral. Purchases and other transactions take place on the third party's website and are governed by its terms and privacy policy.

Retention and requests

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to operate the listing and moderation service, maintain security and address disputes or abuse. To request correction or removal of a submission or ask a privacy question, use the contact information provided on the ZOVARO website.

Security and changes

ZOVARO uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the service evolves; the current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ZOVARO through the contact information provided on the website.

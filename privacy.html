Privacy Policy

ZOVARO helps visitors discover products, offers, job opportunities and community marketplace listings. This policy describes information processed through listing, moderation, reporting and appeal features.

Information submitted

Job submissions may include the employer or company name, company website, role, employment type, workplace, location, description, compensation information where supplied, contact email and an authorization declaration. Product submissions may include the seller's display or business name, seller country, seller type, product details, condition, price, optional external image URL, contact email and declarations about authority to sell and ownership.

Automated moderation

ZOVARO uses automated rule-based screening to identify prohibited or suspicious content, common job-scam patterns, incomplete seller information and categories that require additional review. Depending on the screening result, a listing may be published automatically, rejected automatically or held privately for further review. Automated screening is not a legal determination and does not verify a person's identity, a business, product authenticity, ownership, safety or the accuracy of claims. Automated decisions may be appealed using the appeal option shown after a rejection. ZOVARO may also review listings manually and change a decision.

Publication and contact details

Submissions are stored in access-controlled private storage. Only approved listings are displayed publicly. A contact email is displayed only where the submitter has expressly consented to public display. Seller display names and listing information may be published when the listing is approved. Do not submit another person's personal information without authorization, and do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information in descriptions.

Reports and appeals

Visitors may report a listing they believe is unlawful, unsafe, fraudulent or otherwise violates the rules. Submitters may appeal certain moderation decisions. Reports and appeals are stored privately and used to assess the relevant listing and improve service safety. Contact details supplied with a report or appeal are used to administer that case.

Purposes and service providers

Information is used to receive and display listings, administer automated and human moderation, prevent abuse, handle reports and appeals, secure the service and respond to legal or safety issues. ZOVARO uses hosting and private storage providers and may use affiliate-network and social-media services. Those providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Affiliate links and external websites

ZOVARO may display affiliate links to third-party merchants. When a visitor follows a link, the merchant or affiliate network may receive technical information needed to process the referral. Purchases and other transactions take place on third-party websites and are governed by their terms and privacy policies.

Retention and privacy requests

Information is retained as reasonably necessary to operate listings, moderation, reports, appeals, security and dispute handling, subject to applicable law. To request access, correction or deletion, or ask a privacy question, use the contact information published on the ZOVARO website. Requests may require verification and may be subject to legal retention obligations.

Security and updates

ZOVARO uses reasonable technical and organizational measures, including private storage for pending submissions and moderation records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as the service evolves.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ZOVARO through the contact information provided on the website.
